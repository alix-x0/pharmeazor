## Unclassified (42229-5)

Drug Facts

## Active ingredient (in each caplet) (55106-9)

Acetaminophen 500 mg

## Purpose (55105-1)

Pain reliever/fever reducer

## Indications and Usage (34067-9)

temporarily relieves minor aches and pains due to:

the common cold
headache
backache
minor pain of arthritis
toothache
muscular aches
premenstrual and menstrual cramps

temporarily reduces fever

## Unclassified (42229-5)

This product contains acetaminophen. Severe liver damage may occur if you take

more than 4,000 mg of acetaminophen in 24 hours
with other drugs containing acetaminophen
3 or more alcoholic drinks every day while using this product

## Unclassified (42229-5)

acetaminophen may cause severe skin reactions. Symptoms may include:

skin reddening
blisters
rash

If a skin reaction occurs, stop use and seek medical help right away.

## Do not use (50570-1)

with any other drug containing acetaminophen (prescription or nonprescription). If you are not sure whether a drug contains acetaminophen, ask a doctor or pharmacist.
if you are allergic to acetaminophen or any of the inactive ingredients in this product

## OTC - ASK DOCTOR SECTION (50569-3)

Ask a doctor before use if you have liver disease.

## OTC - ASK DOCTOR/PHARMACIST SECTION (50568-5)

Ask a doctor or pharmacist before use if you are taking the blood thinning drug warfarin

## Stop use and ask a doctor if (50566-9)

pain gets worse or lasts more than 10 days
fever gets worse or lasts more than 3 days
new symptoms occur
redness or swelling is present

These could be signs of a serious condition.

## OTC - PREGNANCY OR BREAST FEEDING SECTION (53414-9)

If pregnant or breast-feeding, ask a health professional before use.

## Unclassified (42229-5)

In case of overdose, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away (1-800-222-1222). Quick medical attention is critical for adults as well as for children even if you do not notice any signs or symptoms.
